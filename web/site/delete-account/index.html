Account & data

Delete Your Homaclo Account & Data

You can ask us to permanently delete your Homaclo account and the personal data linked to it. Homaclo uses one account for the Homaclo website (homaclo.in) and the Homaclo Android app, so one request removes your account from both.

To delete your account, send us an email

Write to homaclo.official@gmail.com from the email address you use with Homaclo and ask us to delete your account.

✉ Email homaclo.official@gmail.com

How to request deletion

  1. Send an emailEmail homaclo.official@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Homaclo account”. Please write from your registered email address and mention your name and registered phone number.
  2. We verify your requestTo protect your account, we may reply to confirm that the request really comes from you.
  3. We delete your accountYour account and data are deleted within 7 working days and we confirm by email. The deletion applies to the website and the app together.

What gets deleted

Deleted permanently

  • Profile details: name, email, phone number and profile photo
  • Saved addresses and location details
  • Booking history and service preferences
  • Reviews you submitted
  • Coins, coupons and referral rewards
  • Notifications and support tickets

May be kept for a limited time

  • Payment and transaction records that we must keep for accounting, tax and legal reasons under Indian law (payments are processed by Razorpay)
  • Records needed to prevent fraud or to resolve a dispute

These are kept only for the period the law requires and are not used for anything else.

Please note: deleting your account cannot be undone. Unused coins and coupons are lost, and any upcoming booking should be completed or cancelled before you send the request.

Questions?

Write to us at homaclo.official@gmail.com. You can also read how we handle your data in our Privacy & Refund Policy.

Need help instead?

If you only want to fix a problem with a booking, we are happy to help.

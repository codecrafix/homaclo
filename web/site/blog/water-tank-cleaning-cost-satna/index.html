What decides the cost?

  • Tank capacity: the main factor. Check the litre capacity printed on your tank or in your builder’s papers.
  • Number of tanks: homes often have a roof tank plus an underground sump, and flats may have several.
  • Type of tank: Homaclo cleans overhead tanks and underground sumps for homes, apartments and businesses.

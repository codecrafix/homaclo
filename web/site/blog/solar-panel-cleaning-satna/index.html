Why dirty solar panels produce less power

Solar panels work by turning sunlight into electricity. Dust, dried mud, pollen and bird droppings sit on the glass and block part of that light. Even partial shading from droppings can cut the output of a panel more than you would expect. Rain washes off some dust, but it often leaves streaks and does not remove stubborn dirt. Satna’s dry, dusty summers and nearby industrial activity mean panels can get dirty quickly.

How often should you clean solar panels?

  • Every 1 to 3 months is a common guide in dusty areas. Your installer’s manual is the best reference for your system.
  • After dust storms or a long dry spell.
  • Soon after bird droppings, because they dry hard and cast a strong shadow.
  • Before peak sunny months, so panels are clean when they matter most.

Safe solar panel cleaning tips

  1. Clean in the early morning or evening, when panels are cool. Cold water on hot glass is a bad mix.
  2. Start with a gentle rinse of clean water to lift loose dust.
  3. Use a soft cloth or soft brush. Avoid abrasive scrubbers and harsh chemicals.
  4. Do not use a high-pressure washer on panels.
  5. Never step on panels and take care on the roof. If access is difficult, leave it to a professional.

When to call a professional

Rooftop work carries a fall risk, and large arrays take time. If your panels are hard to reach, heavily soiled or you suspect a fault such as a drop in output, a professional visit is safer. Frequently asked questions

How often should solar panels be cleaned?

As a general guide, every one to three months in dusty areas, and after dust storms or bird droppings. Follow your installer’s advice for your system.

Can I clean solar panels with a hose?

A gentle rinse with clean water on cool panels removes loose dust. Avoid pressure washers and abrasive scrubbers, which can damage the glass.

Why water tank cleaning matters

Your tank stores the water your family drinks, cooks with and bathes in. Over time, dust, sediment and sludge settle at the bottom, and damp walls can grow algae. In a dusty city like Satna, an uncovered or poorly sealed tank collects even more. Regular cleaning keeps stored water fresh and protects your pipes and filters from clogging.

A simple tank cleaning schedule

  • Every 6 months: a sensible default for most homes, flats and shops.
  • Every 3 to 4 months: consider this if your tank is exposed to a lot of dust, sits near construction work, or you notice sediment often.
  • After the monsoon: heavy rain and humidity can speed up slime and algae growth.
  • After a long absence or new construction: if the house was empty for weeks, or you just moved in, start with a clean tank.
  • Immediately: if the water smells, changes colour or tastes odd. See our guide to the signs your water tank needs cleaning.

Overhead tank vs underground sump

Most homes in Satna have both: an underground sump that stores incoming supply and an overhead tank on the roof that feeds your taps. Clean both. If only the roof tank is cleaned, dirty water from the sump refills it straight away.

What professional tank cleaning involves

  1. The tank is emptied and the sludge at the bottom is removed with a wet-and-dry vacuum.
  2. Inner walls are scrubbed and washed with a high-pressure washer.
  3. Inlet and outlet pipes are flushed so nothing is left in the line.
  4. The tank is rinsed and refilled with fresh water.
  5. A TDS meter reading is taken so you can see the result. TDS shows dissolved solids, so it is a quick visible check and not a full laboratory test.

Why not clean it yourself?

You can, but rooftop tanks mean ladders, slippery walls and tight openings, and it is hard to remove fine sludge properly with a bucket and brush. Frequently asked questions

How often should I clean my overhead water tank?

As a general rule, at least once every six months, and sooner if the water smells, looks cloudy or shows sediment.

Do I need to clean the underground sump too?

Yes. The sump feeds your overhead tank, so a dirty sump can contaminate a freshly cleaned tank. Homaclo cleans both overhead tanks and underground sumps.

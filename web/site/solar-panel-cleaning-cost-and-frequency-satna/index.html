Why clean solar panels at all?

Panels make power from sunlight, so anything that blocks light costs you units. A layer of dust, dried mud or bird droppings reduces the light that reaches the cells. Industry sources, such as an Energetica India article from May to June 2026, estimate that consistently clean panels can produce roughly 10 to 20 percent more energy over a year than poorly maintained ones. The real gap depends on your location, season, tilt and how dirty the panels get, so treat it as a range, not a promise.

Per-panel pricing vs yearly packages

A yearly package gives you scheduled visits at a lower cost per visit, which suits people who do not want to remember to book. Per-panel pricing suits you if you only want a clean after a dusty spell, bird droppings or construction nearby, and want to pay only for what you use. Pick whichever matches how dirty your panels actually get.

How often should you clean?

There is no single schedule. Published recommendations range from about four cleanings a year for basic packages to every two weeks in very dusty places. These things push you towards cleaning more often:

  • Dust and traffic: panels near an unpaved road, a construction site or a dry open plot.
  • Bird droppings and leaves: trees close to the roof, or a favourite perch near the panels.
  • Low tilt: flatter panels hold dust longer than steeper ones.
  • Dry season: long gaps between rain let dust build up.

A practical rule for most homes: look at the panels once a month (from a safe place, or compare the generation figure on your inverter or app), and book a clean when you see a visible layer or after a long dry, dusty spell. If a hazy film shows up on a sunny day or generation drops with no other reason, cleaning is the first and cheapest thing to try.

Does rain clean solar panels?

Partly. Rain washes off loose dust, but it often leaves mud streaks and does not remove dried droppings, so panels can still be dirty after a shower.

Cleaning mistakes to avoid

  • Cleaning at midday when panels are hot. Wait for early morning or evening.
  • Using abrasive pads, steel wool or strong chemicals.
  • Aiming a high-pressure jet at panels, wiring or connectors.
  • Walking on panels. Pressure can cause tiny cracks you cannot see.
  • Ignoring your installer or manufacturer’s instructions. Improper cleaning can damage panels and may affect warranty claims.

Frequently asked questions

How much does solar panel cleaning cost in Satna?

Homaclo charges ₹100 per panel for 3 kW to 30 kW systems, with a minimum of 5 panels. For example, 10 panels cost 10 × ₹100 = ₹1,000.

How often should solar panels be cleaned?

It depends on dust, nearby construction, bird droppings and rain. A simple approach is to inspect the panels by eye every month and clean when you see a layer of dust or droppings.

Does rain clean solar panels?

Rain can wash away some loose dust, but it often leaves mud marks and does not remove dried bird droppings, so panels can still need cleaning after rain.

Is it safe to clean solar panels myself?

It can be, if you use a soft cloth or brush and clean water, clean when the panels are cool, avoid high pressure and never walk on the panels. If reaching the roof is risky, book a professional.

Can cleaning void my solar warranty?

Improper cleaning, such as abrasive tools or high pressure, can damage panels and may affect warranty claims. Check your installer or panel manufacturer instructions before you clean.
